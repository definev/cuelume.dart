## 0.1.0

* First Flutter port of [Cuelume](https://github.com/Danilaa1/cuelume) 0.2.2.
* Twenty-one live-synthesized interaction sounds, no audio files, including
  focus, attention, notification, and cancel cues.
* `Cuelume.play`, `setEnabled`, `setVolume`, and `CuelumeListener`.
* SoLoud playback adapter behind an internal seam (see `docs/adr/0001-flutter-port-architecture.md`).
